# IXD-404 W8 — Buttons

Two button sets, each with its own tone. Both have primary, secondary and tertiary buttons, a single-selection group, and default, hover, pressed and disabled states. Press **S** (or **spec**) on either page to open its specimen sheet with measurements.

- **Château** (`chateau.html`): a house of cards. Every card in your hand is a button. Play them in a row to make poker hands, chain scores before the timer runs out, and fill a 15-card tower.
- **Eiche** (`eiche.html`): an oak on a hill. Acorns, leaves, the wind word, wind shapes and a grove ledger.
